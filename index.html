What is the natural voice?

Kokoro v1.0 is an open-weight, 82-million-parameter speech model. Hear uses its mobile-friendly 8-bit edition in a background worker, so generation stays responsive and private.

The five choices are voice profiles inside the same model—not five separate downloads. Model files are cached by Safari; the text and generated audio stay on this device.
